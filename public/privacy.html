Bellmont City — Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What this covers

This policy covers the game Bellmont City: the website https://bellmont-city.web.app, the Android app and the Windows app (both open the same website).

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address (if you sign up with email or Google), your username, your password (stored only by Google's sign-in service, never readable by us), and your profile picture (one of the game's pictures, or your Google picture if you choose it).
  • Your game: your saved progress (your city life: money, home, items, levels and so on), the rooms you create or join, and things you leave in shared rooms.
  • Friends and chats: your friends list, friend requests, room invitations, and the private messages you send to friends.
  • Notifications: if you turn notifications on, an address for your device (a Firebase Cloud Messaging token on Android, or a browser push address on Windows) and your language, so notifications can reach that device.
  • While playing in a room: your character's position and actions are shared live with the other players in that room.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, we show no ads, and we have no in-app purchases.

How it's used

Only to run the game: signing you in, saving your progress, letting you play with friends, delivering your messages, and sending the notifications you asked for. We never sell your data or share it with anyone for advertising.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (sign-in, database and website hosting). Notifications are sent through a small Cloudflare service, which keeps only your devices' notification addresses. Both are bound by their own privacy and security commitments.

Who can see what

  • Other players can see your username and profile picture.
  • Only you and the friend you're writing to can read your private messages.
  • Players in the same room see your character and what it does there.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time, in the game: open the menu (your picture, top left) → Settings → Delete account → Delete my account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, your saved progress, your username, your email address, your friends list and requests, the messages you sent, and your devices' notification addresses. It can't be undone.

If you can no longer sign in to the game, contact the developer through the contact details on the app's Google Play page and ask for your account to be deleted; it will be done within 30 days.

Children

Bellmont City is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
